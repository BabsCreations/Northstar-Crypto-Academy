Begin with understanding

Before the first trade,
learn the language.

A solid foundation helps you ask better questions. Here are a few ideas worth understanding before you step into the market.

01 / ASSET₿

Bitcoin

A digital asset introduced in 2009, designed to let people transfer value on a peer-to-peer network. Its supply rules and decentralized design set it apart from traditional currencies.

A question to explore ↗
02 / NETWORK◇

Ethereum

A blockchain network that supports programmable agreements, often called smart contracts, as well as its own native asset, ether.

03 / SYSTEM▤

Blockchain

A shared record of transactions maintained across a network. Participants use agreed rules to validate and add new entries.

04 / REALITY〰

Volatility

Prices can change sharply and unpredictably. Large moves can happen in either direction, and losses are possible.

05 / CONTEXT⌁

How markets work

Markets bring buyers and sellers together. Price reflects their changing expectations, available liquidity, and new information. No single signal tells the whole story.

Participants→Orders→Price discovery

Good decisions start
before the chart.

A repeatable process can help you stay grounded when markets get noisy. No method removes uncertainty; preparation can help you meet it.

Keep perspective

Protect your ability
to keep learning.

Risk management cannot make a risky market safe. It can help you set boundaries and avoid decisions that put more at stake than you intended.

01

Set a loss limit

Consider in advance how much you could lose without affecting essential expenses or financial goals.

02

Treat leverage with caution

Borrowed exposure can magnify losses as well as gains, and positions can be liquidated quickly.

03

Question certainty

Promises of easy, guaranteed, or unusually high returns are a reason to pause and investigate.

04

Take a breath

Fear of missing out and urgency can cloud judgment. You can always step back and learn more.

!Only consider risk you understand and can afford to take.

Start here, then keep exploring. A thoughtful learner is always allowed to ask one more question.

Is this financial advice?

No. Northstar Crypto Academy shares general educational information only. It does not consider your financial situation and is not personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

How much money do I need to begin learning?

You can begin learning without buying any crypto. Focus on understanding the concepts and risks first. Never use money needed for essentials or take on debt to speculate.

Can I lose money trading crypto?

Yes. Crypto assets can be highly volatile, and you could lose some or all of the money you commit. Leverage, platform issues, scams, and mistakes can add further risks.

What should I research before considering an asset?

Explore what the project is designed to do, how its network and token work, who maintains it, how it may be used, and what security or governance risks exist. Seek independent, current sources and stay alert to conflicts of interest.

Do I need to trade to make progress?

No. Learning the landscape, practicing research, and understanding your own risk tolerance are meaningful steps. Choosing not to trade is always valid.
